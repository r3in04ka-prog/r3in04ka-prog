## Example: meeting the kitten

**User:** someone who downloaded Purrsonal but has not connected a model.

**Goal:** understand the interface and find its features.

**Journey:** open the kitten → Try demo → ask a sample question → see a reply clearly labelled as a demo.

**Included:** one entry button, scripted replies and an explicit exit to model settings.

**Out of scope:** open-ended AI conversation, voice and autonomous computer control.

**Done when:** the demo works without a model or network request, and switching modes preserves the saved key and settings.

**Still unproven:** real-model response quality and usability for all users require separate checks.
